Research work at HKUST's Cheng Kar-Shun Robotics Institute.

- Brought up an **AgileX PIPER 6-DoF arm** on a **Scout Mini UGV** in ROS 2 (CAN bus, Nav2, SLAM Toolbox).
- 2D-LiDAR localisation with custom reflective markers; **MoveIt 2** motion planning.
- Radar-triggered chess pick-and-place demo (Acconeer A121) that ran **8+ hours live** at the HKUST AI Film Festival.
