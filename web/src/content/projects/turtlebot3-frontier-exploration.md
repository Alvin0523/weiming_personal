Competition task: map an unknown indoor arena in the shortest time.

- **TurtleBot3 Waffle** with LiDAR, SLAM Toolbox and Nav2 on ROS 2 Jazzy with Zenoh middleware.
- Custom **frontier-exploration node** to pick the next area to explore.
- Tested in Gazebo on the competition arena, then on the real robot.
