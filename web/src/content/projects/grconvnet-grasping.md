Course competition: pick up 10 different objects with a robot arm.

- Grasped 10 objects with an **AIRBOT Play** arm and RGB-D camera using **GR-ConvNet v2** trained on Jacquard V2.
- Camera-to-robot extrinsic calibration (PnP) and IK-based grasp execution.
- Prototyped the pipeline in **MuJoCo** (DISCOVERSE) before running it on the real arm.
